Hi, I'm Diana. I'm a former architect and spent several years balancing my personal project as a jewelry designer with administrative and customer support work.

Upon turning 40, I felt a strong desire for change, for learning new skills to find a more functional and fulfilling work solution. So, I decided to give myself a second chance and prepare for the second part of my life!

Going backb to the beginning, I went back to school. I took a UX & UI design course at Edit.
I particularly enjoyed the user experience discipline for its methodology and way of thinking centered on empathy with the user. All research phases were also fascinating - but the design thinking approach as a group discussion strategy was, undoubtedly, one of my favorite experiences.

With the user interface discipline I learned to think better about the interaction between human beings and a digital tool or machne, and how the good use of visual design can be crucial.

While exploring theses paths, I realized i could gain knowledge about how the "machine" works - in this case a web product - and decided to take a Front-End Web Developer course at Flag.

Following this learning trajectory, I felt the need to take another important step and left my administrative job to dedicate myself fully to my studies.

I learned HTML, CSS, Javascript.
With more available, I took an observational drawing course at the drawing club. After all, drawing has always been the first fundamental tool, present from the beginning .

To diversify my knowledge in web development, I am taking a Low-Code Web Developer course at Cesae Digital.

I also started studying Artificial Intelligence on IBM - Coursera to keep up with the pace of the digital world.
